Methodology

This mixes several different water metrics, same as the original: on-site consumptive use (data centers), estimated consumptive use from irrigation × efficiency ratios (farms), estimated consumptive use from deliveries minus sewer returns (cities), modeled withdrawal × consumption ratios (industries), and total withdrawals (national totals). Items sharing a metric type are directly comparable; figures across types show scale, not a like-for-like comparison. Every figure is year-stamped. This section summarizes Masley's published methodology in our own words — for the complete original analysis and every citation, see the full methodology on his site.

Data centers

All US data centers (2023): 17.4B gal/yr direct site water, from LBNL's 2024 data center energy usage report (66B liters). ChatGPT and "all US AI workloads" figures are modeled estimates built from OpenAI's disclosed per-message energy/water figures (via OpenAI's usage data and Sam Altman's blog) and traffic scaled by weekly-active-user growth, cross-checked against Google's and Microsoft's own reported figures (Google, Microsoft). They are not OpenAI-disclosed totals.

Other industries

Modeled from EPA effluent-guideline and industry-association benchmarks, using withdrawal-to-consumption ratios rather than direct utility disclosures — e.g. EPA meat & poultry, FDA pharmaceutical water use, Brewers Association, and semiconductor fab water use. These are plausible modeled estimates, not first-party disclosures, and public-supply-potable shares are uncertain for several of them.

City water systems & national totals

City figures estimate consumption as deliveries minus sewer returns, using each utility's own reporting (NYC, Chicago, LA, Phoenix, Las Vegas/SNWA). National totals apply 1995 USGS sector-level consumptive fractions to newer withdrawal data.

Agriculture & lawns/turf

Crop figures estimate consumptive use as applied irrigation × an efficiency ratio, from USDA's Farm & Ranch Irrigation Survey and state extension publications. Lawn irrigation and golf-course figures are modeled from EPA WaterSense and GCSAA's golf course water survey.

Electricity-related water ("+ elec")

Where an item has a "+ elec" option, it adds the water consumed at power plants to generate that sector's electricity, using a thermoelectric-only factor of 0.47 gal/kWh (NREL), deliberately excluding hydroelectric reservoir evaporation — per Construction Physics's argument that reservoir evaporation happens regardless of the electricity demand driving it. A convention that includes hydro (LBNL's) would roughly 2.5× the electricity-water component. Public-supply-potable ("treated") mode doesn't split out a separate elec-inclusive figure, so the "+ elec" option is only available in "All source water" mode here.

Key caveats

This mixes incompatible metrics by necessity — see the intro above. The ChatGPT/AI-workload figures are modeled, not disclosed, and are the shakiest numbers here. Microsoft and Google figures are global operational totals, not US-only or datacenter-only. Public-supply-potable shares are genuinely uncertain for several industrial categories. Full caveats and the complete numeric derivations are in the original methodology.

Water Scale Indicator tiers (Individual / Organizational / City / Industrial / Agricultural / National) describe the type and level of aggregation of each figure — not a judgment of harm or severity.
