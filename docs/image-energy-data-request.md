# Draft request for image-generation measurement data

Subject: Request for companion data for “The Hidden Cost of an Image”

Hello Dr. Bertazzini and coauthors,

I am building an educational, locally running environmental-footprint calculator and would like to reproduce the energy results from your paper, “The Hidden Cost of an Image: Quantifying the Energy Consumption of AI Image Generation” (arXiv:2506.17016), without estimating values from chart pixels.

Could you share the companion dataset or a machine-readable table containing the image-generation energy observations for each of the 17 models at 512 × 512, 768 × 1024, and 1024 × 1024? Raw observations would be ideal; per-cell minimum/lower bound, median, and maximum/upper bound with the aggregation rule would also work. Please also include the data's reuse license and identify whether warm-up runs, quantization variants, and prompt-length groups should be filtered or pooled to reproduce the paper's reported summaries.

The arXiv source package refers to companion material, but I could not find a link or numeric table in that package. I will preserve the study boundary and limitations, cite the paper and dataset, and will not represent these open-model workstation measurements as measurements of proprietary hosted services.

Thank you for making this research available.
